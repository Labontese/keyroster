<objective>
Put the signer under the root-signed trust bundle: `ca-init` creates the separate user, host and machine CAs (plus ops and log keys), `install-bundle` verifies the bundle and genesis policy against pinned roots, `serve` takes all keys from the bundle, and every issuance needs admin SSHSIG evidence from the policy (D-13).

Purpose: CA-01 (separate keys and role binding), CA-04/05/07 completion, KEY-07 enforcement on the signer, D-13, VIS-01. This replaces the skeleton's flag-based pins. Plan 01-08 proves the three CAs separate against real sshd and anchors `audit verify` on the pinned roots (the split keeps each plan inside the context budget).
Output: two signer subcommands, evidence check, policy profiles, the bootstrapSigner e2e helper, the trust-flow e2e, and a PR that passes the merge gate.
</objective>
