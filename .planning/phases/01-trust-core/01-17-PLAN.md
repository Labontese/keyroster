The rule this plan implements is read from `internal/trust/verify.go` `VerifySuccessor` and not invented:
- version prev+1;
- prev = SHA-256 of the previous canonical bundle;
- issued_at not earlier than the previous bundle's;
- policy_sha256 = SHA-256 of the policy;
- the policy is byte-identical to the previous policy, or is version previous+1 with the previous policy's SHA-256 as its prev;
- no policy admin is a root of the next or the previous set (`CheckAdminsNotRoots(p, next roots, prev roots)`);
- at least `prev.Root.Threshold` distinct previous roots AND at least `next.Root.Threshold` distinct new roots signed both the bundle and the policy.

## Edge Coverage

- **Which roots may sign:** a previous root, a new root, or neither (refused). Covered by the previous_root_signs_with_key, new_root_signs_with_key and unknown_root_refused cases.
- **Threshold split:** only new roots signed or only previous roots signed. Both are refused by VerifySuccessor (TestBuildSuccessor round trips) and, end to end, by install-bundle (e2e).
- **Ordering:** a ceremony clock earlier than the previous issued_at is refused (clock_before_prev_refused). The signer refuses install-bundle while serve holds the lock (e2e).
- **Policy:** unchanged, chained v2, same version changed, v+2 and wrong prev are all named cases.
- **Not exercised here:** a FIDO, PIV or PKCS#11 previous root signing a successor through ssh-agent. The agent path is the genesis path plus rootByFingerprint's second set, and the SUMMARY labels it UNVERIFIED.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Operator files → `root sign --prev` | The previous bundle, the policy and roots.pub are operator-supplied files, possibly carried on a transfer USB |
| Root key → successor bundle | A root signature over a successor transfers trust from the previous root set to the new one |
| Successor bundle → signer (`install-bundle`) | A successor is verified against the installed bundle only (no pins) |
