<objective>
Put the signer under the root-signed trust bundle: `ca-init` creates the separate user, host and machine CAs (plus ops and log keys), `install-bundle` verifies the bundle and genesis policy against pinned roots, `serve` takes all keys from the bundle, every issuance needs admin SSHSIG evidence from the policy (D-13), and `audit verify` anchors on the pinned roots.

Purpose: CA-01, CA-04/05/07 completion, KEY-07 enforcement on the signer, D-13, VIS-01/03 completion. This replaces the skeleton's flag-based pins.
Output: two signer subcommands, evidence check, policy profiles, bundle-anchored verify, full trust-flow e2e, PR.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Admin group member → signer | A process in keyroster-admin may still be compromised; it must also hold an admin key from the root-signed policy |
| Transfer media → install-bundle | Bundles arrive from the offline ceremony and are untrusted until verified against pins |
| Export → audit verifier | The verifier anchors on pinned roots, not on anything in the export |
