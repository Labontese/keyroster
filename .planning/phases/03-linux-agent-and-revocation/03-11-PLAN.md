## Edge Coverage (spec-less fallback, this plan)

**Flagged assumption, AUTH-06 (unclassified, unresolved).** Assumed: "day-scale within role caps" means the smallest of the machine entry's TTL, its roles' TTLs and the machine CA cap, and an automation renews before expiry on its own schedule. A clock-skewed automation host is bounded by the signer's ±300 s freshness window; a scheduler that misses renewals simply loses access until the next successful renew, which is the intended fail-safe direction.
