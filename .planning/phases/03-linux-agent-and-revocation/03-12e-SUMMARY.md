# Phase 03 Plan 12e: CA rotation, switch binding and fences Summary

A role's CA switch (its `next` key active, its old key `draining` until an immutable `trusted_until` at or above the floor from both policies) is admitted by the shared chain rule and built by `BuildSuccessor`; the signer installs it only behind an off-box checkpoint the log extends and only when the drain end covers every certificate of the old key that the verified log records (carried certificates included), audit re-derives that binding, its upper bound and the issued_at rule, retire-key removes the old key and serve refuses while it is held, and a recovery opens no CA key. The proof here is in-process; the ceremony tool and the real-binary proof are 03-12e2's.
