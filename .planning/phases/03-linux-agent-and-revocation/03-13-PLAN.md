## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-82 | Tampering | rewriting history by swapping the log key | high | mitigate | A log-key change only inside a verified successor; the old key seals its last checkpoint; audit follows segments; Phase 4 witnesses add external anchoring |
| T-03-83 | Spoofing | KRLs signed by a stale or unauthorised ops key | high | mitigate | Agents accept only the ops key of the bundle in force; the signer republishes after the change |
| T-03-84 | Elevation of privilege | a compromised CA continuing to mint accepted certificates | critical | mitigate | Emergency successor retires it, the KRL revokes its key so sshd refuses all its certificates; e2e on both OpenSSH versions |
| T-03-85 | Denial of service | revoking an active CA, ops, log or root key by mistake | high | mitigate | Only a retired CA key may be revoked by key; others refused in signer and audit |
| T-03-86 | Denial of service | a signer that cannot restart after a log-key change | medium | mitigate | Start-up checkpoint check per segment; e2e restarts after rotation |
| T-03-SC | Tampering | dependencies | low | accept | No module change |
</threat_model>
