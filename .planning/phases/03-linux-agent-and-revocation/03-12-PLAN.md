<objective>
Rotate a CA key with no failed logins (KEY-08, D-14): prepare a new generation, trust old and new in parallel until the fleet has converged, switch with a drain end date, and let agents drop the old CA on their own; proven end to end with a login loop that never fails.

Output: keystore generations for every backend, the generations table and leaf kind 14, bundle `draining` and `trusted_until`, successor rules and `root sign` transition flags, signer and agent handling, known-hosts with next and draining host CAs, the e2e rotation test, a TPM lane case, vectors, the CA rotation runbook, and a PR. Log-key and ops-key rotation and the emergency path follow in 03-13.
</objective>
